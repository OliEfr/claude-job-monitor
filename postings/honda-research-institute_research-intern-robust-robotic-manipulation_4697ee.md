### Research Intern: Robust Robotic Manipulation

Job Number: P26INT-26

Honda Research Institute USA (HRI-US) is seeking a self-motivated Ph.D. student to join the Intelligence Robotics Research Division as a summer intern. The intern will conduct research on robust, contact-rich dexterous manipulation, with a focus on methods that detect, prevent, and recover from failures of learned policies.

SUMMER – San Jose, CA

Key Responsibilities

- Formulate and develop novel algorithms for robust manipulation, including but not limited to policy performance characterization, failure reasoning, post-training (e.g., RL fine-tuning), and recovery policy methods.

- Develop methods that utilize multimodal sensing (vision, proprioception, tactile) to prevent and recover from policy failures.

- Implement and validate methods in simulation and on real robot hardware.

- Contribute to HRI's patent portfolio and publish research results when applicable at top-tier conferences and journals in robotics and machine learning. 

- Communicate progress through presentations and written reports.

Minimum Qualifications

- Currently enrolled in a Ph.D. program in Computer Science, Robotics, Electrical or Computer Engineering, Machine Learning, or a related field.

- Strong background in robot learning and core robotics concepts (e.g., kinematics, dynamics, control).

- Research experience in at least one relevant area, such as failure detection, recovery methods, or policy fine-tuning.

- Proficiency in Python and experience with robotics physics simulators (e.g., MuJoCo, Isaac Sim).

- Ability to independently formulate research questions, design experiments, and analyze results.

- Strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to work effectively in a collaborative research environment.

Bonus Qualifications

- Experience with reinforcement learning, imitation learning, multimodal learning, and/or state estimation.

- Experience deploying learned policies on real robots, including sim-to-real transfer and real-world data collection.

- Experience with dexterous multi-fingered hands, contact-rich manipulation, and/or tactile sensing.

- Publications at relevant venues such as ICRA, IROS, CoRL, RSS, CVPR, NeurIPS, etc.
