### OR0354: Internship - Humanoid Dexterous Manipulation
 
- The Optimizations and Intelligent Robotics team at MERL is seeking a highly motivated intern to conduct fundamental research on dexterous humanoid robot manipulation. The project covers multiple research topics including multi-finger dexterous manipulation, tactile/force-sensing, object handover, robot learning, contact modeling, and is targeting specific humanoid robot tasks. 
Applicants must be Ph.D. students with a strong background in robot learning such as VLAs, diffusion and RL. The selected intern will collaborate closely with MERL researchers to design and implement novel algorithms, conduct experiments, and disseminate research findings in one of the top-tier robotics conferences and patents. The internship duration and start date are flexible. Interested applicants are encouraged to apply with an updated CV and a list of relevant publications.
 
Required Specific Experience 
 
- Current enrollment in a Ph.D. program in Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field. 
- Hands-on research experience with learning models for robotics (Diffusion, RL, imitation learning, VLAs etc) 
- Dexterous manipulation using humanoid hands or bimanual manipulation. 
- Contact-rich manipulation and force-controlled manipulation. 
- Experience with tactile or force/torque sensing 
- Robotic simulators (Isaac Lab, MuJoCo, Genesis, etc). 
- Programming skills in at least one common language (C++, Python, etc) 
- Strong publication record or demonstrated research potential in robotics, embodied AI, computer vision, or machine learning. Must have some publications in top robotic or AI conferences or journals (i.e., RSS, ICRA, IROS, T-RO, RA-L, NeurIPS, CoRL, ICML etc).
The pay range for this internship position will be 6-8K per month.

- Research Areas: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics
