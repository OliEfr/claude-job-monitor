Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis; we encourage early submission. The anticipated application window is open until July 16, 2027, but may close earlier if all available projects are full.

-
This application is intended for candidates who will be enrolled in a PhD degree program at the time of the engagement and have a demonstrated research focus.

-
Unlike the Research Internship, which is a structured seasonal placement, the Student Researcher role offers greater flexibility in weekly time commitment, project duration, and onsite/remote options. Specific work arrangements and engagement timelines are confirmed during the offer stage.

-
This program is intended for students who will continue their academic studies. Participation in this program does not guarantee automatic full-time hiring consideration. Individuals interested in pursuing a full-time role at Google following their Student Researcher engagement are encouraged to apply through standard external hiring channels.

-
Participation in this program requires that you are located in the United States for the duration of the engagement, though select remote arrangements from within the United States or Canada may be permitted depending on project requirements. Candidates residing in other countries must relocate to the United States for the engagement.

-
Because projects are approved on an ongoing basis throughout the application cycle, application review takes place continuously and may take several months. You will be contacted directly if your application is selected to advance in the hiring process.

To be considered, please provide:

-
Updated CV or Resume: You must include your anticipated graduation date (MM/YY).

-
Transcripts: A current unofficial or official transcript in English (PDF preferred).

-
Research Focus: Highlighting publications, specialized lab work, or significant research contributions is highly recommended.

Start dates for this role are typically between January and August 2027. Applications submitted after the window closes or once all projects are filled will not be considered.

The Student Researcher Program fosters academic collaborations by hiring students onto research projects aligned to company priorities in scientific advancement. The program offers placements on teams across Google, for research, engineering, and science roles. As a Student Researcher, you will have the opportunity to participate in research projects focused on developing solutions for real-world, large-scale problems.

Student Researcher projects are exploratory and experiences that drive scientific advancement across a multitude of research areas. Students will work collaboratively on projects that explore innovative research challenges and support the creation of breakthrough technologies.

Projects vary in duration and location based on team and student requirements. It is required that you are located in one of the specific country locations identified for this role for the full duration of the engagement. When you apply, you will be considered for Student Researcher positions across all of Google's research teams including DeepMind, Google Research, Google Cloud and more. This allows us to find the right project match for your skills and interests.

Researchers across Google are working to advance the state of the art in computing and build the next generation of intelligent systems for all Google products. To achieve this, we invest in foundational research and work on projects that utilize the latest computer science techniques developed by skilled software developers and research scientists. Whether we're shaping the future of sustainability, optimizing algorithms, or pioneering AI systems, our teams strive to continuously progress science, advance society, and improve the lives of billions of people.
