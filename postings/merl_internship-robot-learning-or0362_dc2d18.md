 OR0362: Internship - Robot Learning
 
- 

MERL is seeking an intern to work on machine learning algorithms that enable robots to acquire novel skills and competencies based on collected data from their interaction with the operating environment, either under teleoperation or as a result of autonomous exploration. The research will explore methods for robust handling of variability in task settings and algorithms for autonomous acquisition of primitive skills, task/scene descriptors, and reliable chaining of skills with the objective of performing complex, multi-stage manipulation tasks. The ideal candidate would be a PhD student in robotics, computer science, engineering, applied mathematics, or related fields with a strong background in machine learning, familiarity with industrial robot arms, and excellent coding skills. The intern will work closely with MERL researchers to develop and verify novel algorithms and test them on real robot hardware. The position offers opportunities to combine the invention of novel algorithms with hands-on experimentation, and to prepare results for patents and publication. The internship is expected to last between 3 and 5 months, with a flexible start date after April 1, 2027. 

Required Specific Experience 
 
- Strong academic background in robotics: a focus on manipulation is preferred. 
- Solid coursework in machine learning: a working familiarity with robot learning algorithms, including learning from demonstration and reinforcement learning, is expected. Experience with using and training VLA models is highly desirable. 
- Hands-on experience and good knowledge of computer vision: familiarity with both classical (geometric) methods, as well as machine-learning-based algorithms is expected. 
- Excellent Python coding skills: proven track record of turning scientific ideas into research code. Solid hands-on expertise with git is required and experience with Codex is strongly desired. 
- Research independence: Ability to quickly research the state of the art in specific subareas of robot learning is essential, as well as skills in identifying ideas for new algorithms, formulating research hypotheses, implementing code for their verification, conducting experiments, and communicating the results verbally and in the form of scientific publications. 

The pay range for this internship position will be 6-8K per month.
