### Research Intern: High-Fidelity Robotics Simulation and Learning

Job Number: P26INT-28

Honda Research Institute USA (HRI-US) is seeking a self-motivated Ph.D. student to join our Intelligence Robotics Research Division. The intern will conduct research in high-fidelity robotics simulation, focusing on methods that improve the accuracy, efficiency, and scalability of simulation for complex robotic interactions. The position involves developing novel computational and learning-based approaches that enable advanced robotic applications while addressing the practical limitations of simulation cost and performance. HRI-US welcomes innovative ideas aligned with these broad research areas.

SUMMER – San Jose, CA

Key Responsibilities

- Propose novel ideas and conduct research on the topics described above.

- Formulate research hypotheses, design experiments, and evaluate results.

- Contribute to the portfolio of patents and publish research results when applicable at top-tier conferences and journals.

Minimum Qualifications

- Currently enrolled in a Ph.D. program in computer science, computer graphics, robotics, or a related field.

- A minimum of one year of research experience relevant to the position (e.g., contact-rich policy learning, physics simulation, simulator distillation).

- Programming skills, including sufficient technical depth to critically evaluate and validate outputs from AI-assisted coding or research tools if used.

Bonus Qualifications

- Strong background in physics and mathematics, particularly calculus and linear algebra.

- Experience with contact-rich multi-finger manipulation and understanding of the underlying physical principles.

- Experience designing, implementing, or evaluating model architectures and assessing dataset quality and diversity for robotic manipulation tasks.

- Experience with or understanding of simulation methods and the underlying physics.

- Experience with or understanding of robot dynamics and real-world robotic hardware.
