Research Intern: Robust Planning

Job Number: P25INT-55

Honda Research Institute USA (HRI-US) is seeking a research engineer to develop and implement a novel planning framework. This position requires understanding state-of-the-art planning algorithms and improving their performance under heavy noise and occlusions.

Mountain View, CA

Key Responsibilities

- Research existing state-of-the-art algorithms for robust planning under occlusion.

- Develop a real-time motion planner that can safely and smoothly operate under heavy noise and occlusion.

-

Research and model perception noise.

Minimum Qualifications

- ​M.S. in Robotics or related field.

- Experience with MPC, planning under uncertainty.

- Experience running real-time algorithms on robotic systems or other physical hardware.

Bonus Qualifications​

- Ph.D candidate in Robotics or related field

- Experience with MetaDrive, Waymo Open Motion Dataset, or Driving Data
