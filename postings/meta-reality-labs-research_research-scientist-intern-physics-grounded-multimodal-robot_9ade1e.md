Posted: 2026-11-18 | Location: Redmond, WA

Meta Reality Labs is seeking a Research Scientist Intern with expertise in robot learning, sensor fusion, and real-time state estimation. The ideal candidate will have experience training and adapting robot policies, with a strong background in machine learning, real time state estimation, reconstruction and robotics.
As an intern, you will explore how learned policies and multimodal sensing can improve robotic control and dexterous manipulation. This includes learning object state, physical properties and interaction dynamics, either implicitly within policies or through explicit estimation, to support robust, adaptive robot behavior.

Our internships are twelve (12) to twenty-four (24) weeks long, with various start dates throughout the year.

### Responsibilities

Plan and execute research to advance robotics in the areas of policy learning, sensor fusion, and real-time state estimation. Train new robot policies and fine-tune and apply pretrained models, including vision-language-action (VLA) models/WAM, and policies learned through reinforcement learning (RL) or behavior cloning (BC), for real-time robotic applications. Develop and integrate perception, sensor fusion, and state-estimation methods to support robotic control and manipulation. Collaborate with researchers and engineers to prototype hardware and software solutions, run experiments, and evaluate performance on robotic systems. Work with leading researchers to develop publishable research for top-tier journals and international conferences.

### Qualifications

Currently has, or is in the process of obtaining, a PhD in Robotics, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Computer Science, or a related technical field Research experience in robot learning, perception, state estimation, or control, with a strong foundation in machine learning Proficiency in Python and experience with a deep-learning framework such as PyTorch Research skills involving defining problems, exploring solutions, and analyzing and presenting results Must obtain work authorization in the country of employment at the time of hire and maintain ongoing work authorization during employment Demonstrated research ownership and the ability to conduct cutting-edge research with minimal supervision, along with a proven track record of research contributions through publications at venues such as CoRL, RSS, ICRA, IROS, NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, ICCV or related conferences and journals Experience training new robot policies and fine-tuning pretrained models, including VLA or WAM models and policies trained using reinforcement learning (RL) or behavior cloning (BC) Experience with multimodal sensor fusion, real-time state estimation, tracking, or robotic sensing for dexterous manipulation Experience deploying learned policies on robotic systems, including real-time inference and closed-loop control Experience with physics simulation engines for robotics (such as MuJoCo and SuperDex), sim-to-real transfer, or system identification Experience working and communicating cross-functionally in a research or engineering team Intent to return to the degree program after the internship and availability for at least sixteen (16) consecutive weeks
