### Research Intern: Physical Human-Robot Interaction

Job Number: P26INT-25

Honda Research Institute USA (HRI-US) is seeking a self-motivated Ph.D. student to join our Intelligence Robotics Research Division as a summer intern. The intern will develop and deploy algorithms that enable safe and comfortable physical human-robot interaction (pHRI), including, but not limited to, motion generation for robotic touch, representation learning for safe interaction, and personalized biomechanical modeling of humans.

SUMMER – San Jose, CA

Key Responsibilities

- Develop motion generation algorithms for safe physical robotic interaction with humans.

- Research and develop representations and personalized human models for pHRI, integrating policy learning, biomechanics, and safety.

- Deploy and evaluate the developed algorithms in simulation and on robotic hardware.

- Support experiments for data collection, model training, and algorithm validation.

- Document research methods and findings in internal reports and perform regular presentations.

- Contribute to the portfolio of patents and publish research results at top-tier conferences and journals in human-robot interaction.

Minimum Qualifications

- Ph.D. student in robotics, mechanical engineering, computer science, or a related field.

- Hands-on experience developing and implementing pHRI algorithms on robotic manipulators.

- Strong knowledge of probabilistic modeling, inference methods, and machine learning.

- Proficiency in Python or C++, along with experience using data analysis and visualization tools.

Bonus Qualifications

- Experience with physics simulators (e.g., MuJoCo).

- Experience developing probabilistic models for physical human-robot interaction, such as models of human behavior, biomechanics, safety, or comfort.

- Experience collecting biomechanical data and developing models using MoCap, IMUs, physiological sensors, or similar tools.
