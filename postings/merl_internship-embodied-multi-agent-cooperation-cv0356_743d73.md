 CV0356: Internship - Embodied Multi-Agent Cooperation 
 
- 

MERL is looking for a highly motivated intern to develop a simulation framework for embodied multi-agent cooperation and planning in realistic environments. The ideal candidate should have experience modeling physically realistic and large-scale environments for simulating human-robot interaction scenarios. The intern should also have strong expertise in utilizing the latest AI tools and physics-based simulation software, as well as strong foundations in machine learning and computer vision for training multimodal foundation models. The intern is expected to collaborate with researchers in the computer vision team at MERL to develop algorithms and prepare manuscripts for scientific publications.

Required Specific Experience 
 
- Experience in designing novel realistic 3D interactive scenes for simulated robot learning 
- Experience with extending vision-based embodied AI simulators 
- Strong foundations in machine learning and programming 
- Foundations in optimization, specifically scheduling algorithms, would be a strong plus. 
- Strong track record of publications in top-tier computer vision and machine learning venues (such as CVPR, NeurIPS, etc.) 
- Must be enrolled in a graduate program, ideally towards a Ph.D.

The pay range for this internship position will be 6-8K per month.

- Research Areas: Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Machine Learning
