 CV0357: Internship - Physical AI Reasoning
 
- 

MERL is seeking a highly motivated intern to conduct original research on world modeling for complex physical reasoning. The ideal candidate is a Ph.D. student with a strong background in computer vision, machine learning, and robotics, and must have extensive experience in developing vision-based physical reasoning algorithms. The intern should possess a strong understanding of state-of-the-art physics engines, the ability to model physical dynamics from videos, and expertise in training and utilizing state-of-the-art physics-based foundation models. The intern is expected to collaborate with researchers from multiple teams at MERL to develop algorithms and prepare manuscripts for scientific publications.

Required Specific Experience
 
- Experience with state-of-the-art physics simulators 
- Experience in neuro-physical reasoning approaches 
- Experience in state-of-the-art large vision-and-language models and generative AI models 
- Enrolled in a PhD program 
- Strong track record of publications in top-tier computer vision and machine learning venues (such as CVPR, NeurIPS, etc.).

The pay range for this internship position will be 6-8K per month.
