The Opportunity
We are seeking a research intern to advance spatial intelligence for our robots. Our goal is to enable autonomous long-duration robotic operations in human environments where the robots must understand the physical world in which they operate and adapt as the environment and tasks change.
You will work closely with our robotics and machine learning researchers on a project shaped around your interests, expertise, and the team’s priorities. The work will combine algorithm development with experiments in simulation and on real robots.
The Research
Your project will explore approaches to spatial intelligence such as scene understanding and semantic mapping with the goal of enabling robots to understand their environments and adapt as their environments and that tasks they must perform change.You will work together with researchers developing robot behavior and task-level reasoning to empower the full robotics stack and identify how best to integrate spatial intelligence with task-level and/or agentic reasoning methods.


### Responsibilities 

- 

Work with your mentor to formulate a focused research question, develop an approach, and implement it within the team's research systems.

- 

Develop metrics to assess the performance of robot spatial intelligence and design and run controlled experiments with strong baselines in simulation and on real robots.

- 

Analyze failure modes and maintain reproducible code and experimental records.

- 

Collaborate with researchers and engineers, present findings, and contribute to technical reports and potential publications in robotics and machine learning venues.

### Qualifications 

- 

Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in robotics, machine learning, computer science, or a related field.

- 

Research experience in scene understanding, semantic mapping, or similar areas.

- 

Strong programming skills in Python and/or C++.

- 

Ability to design experiments, reason critically about results, work independently with mentorship, and communicate clearly.

### Bonus Qualifications 

- 

Experience with SLAM and robot mapping/localization/navigation in real-world environments.

- 

Experience with task-level reasoning or agentic methods for robot behavior composition.

- 

Experience with robotics simulation, real-world robot learning, or robot data collection and evaluation systems.

- 

Relevant research contributions demonstrated through publications, open-source projects, or substantial research projects.
