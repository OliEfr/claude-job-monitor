The Team
The Generalist Robot Learning (GRL) team in the Robotics division is looking for research interns for Summer 2027. We are advancing core methods in 3D perception, representation learning, and world modeling. These methods enable robots to understand, reconstruct, simulate, and interact with the world across diverse environments and sensor modalities. Our work spans:
3D and Multi-Modal Foundation Models
Simulation and Co-Training (Sim2Real, Real2Sim, Real2Sim2Real)
World Models (Action-Conditioned World Models, World Action Models, 3D World Models)
Post-Training of robot policies and foundation models (Reinforcement Learning, Reward Modeling)
Our mission is to develop generalizable, data-driven representations and world models that bridge computer vision, graphics, simulation, and robotics. We want them to adapt to novel environments, capture the structure and dynamics of the world, and support robust downstream applications in perception, planning, and interaction.
The Internship
As a Research Intern, you will collaborate with a multidisciplinary team proposing, conducting, and transferring pioneering research at the intersection of Computer Vision, Graphics, Simulation, and Robotics. You will:
Work with large-scale 2D, 3D, and multimodal datasets
Build simulation environments grounded in real-world data
Design and train world models tailored for robotics
Design effective representations and new architectures for generalizable robot learning 
Post-train policies and models with reinforcement learning and learned rewards
Validate ideas in both simulation and physical robotic systems
Your work will aim toward high-impact research publications and contributions to TRI’s mission of enabling robots that can seamlessly perceive, predict, and adapt to the real world.
