Research Intern: Learning Based Motion Planning

Job Number: P25INT-53

Honda Research Institute USA (HRI-US) is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Robotics Software Engineering Intern to work on developing reliable software modules for autonomous driving and cooperative mobility research. Contribute to the development of efficient generative models for end-to-end autonomous driving.

Mountain View, CA

Key Responsibilities

- Train and distill a generative model for the end-to-end autonomous driving task.

- Curate Dataset for generalizable model training.

- Benchmark the developed algorithm's performance in closed loop simulation and on physical hardware.

- Publish findings in a top-tier conference or journal.

Minimum Qualifications

- Research experience in machine learning, decision-making, motion planning, and robotics.

- Excellent programming skills in Python and/or C++, and ROS.

- Experience with ML architectures like Pytorch or Tensorflow.

Bonus Qualifications​

- Publication record in the areas of machine learning, decision-making and motion planning in top-tier venues.
