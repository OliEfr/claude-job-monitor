### Research Intern: Disturbance-Resilient Motion Control for Marine Robotics

Job Number: P26INT-29

Honda Research Institute USA (HRI-US) is seeking a motivated and innovative research intern to develop and validate disturbance-resilient control algorithms for marine robots operating under wind, waves, and currents. The core focus is estimating environmental disturbances and compensating for them using model-based controllers, such as model predictive control (MPC). The algorithms will first be validated in simulation and then on a full-scale test vessel. You will also have the opportunity to explore learning-based control.

SUMMER – San Jose, CA

Key Responsibilities

- Develop disturbance estimation and compensation methods for wind, waves, and currents.

- Implement model-based controllers (e.g., MPC) for trajectory tracking, path following, and station-keeping under environmental disturbances.

- Identify and refine vessel dynamics models from field data.

- Build closed-loop simulations to evaluate robustness before deployment.

- Support deployment and testing of controllers on a full-scale test vessel.

- Explore reinforcement learning or other learning-based control as an extension.

- Document results and contribute to publications.

Minimum Qualifications

- M.S. candidate in robotics, mechanical, electrical, or aerospace engineering, or a related field.

- Solid understanding of model-based control, including model predictive control (MPC).

- Experience implementing control algorithms in simulation or on hardware.

- Proficiency in Python or C++.

Bonus Qualifications

- Ph.D. candidate in a related field.

- Experience with motion control for surface vessels or other marine robots.

- Experience with disturbance estimation/rejection, robust control, or adaptive control.

- Experience with system identification or vessel dynamics modeling.

- Experience with ROS 2.

- Hands-on experience with maritime simulation environments.

- Demonstrated ability to take algorithms beyond simulation, such as field deployment on real hardware.

- Experience with reinforcement learning or other learning-based control methods.

- Publication record in robotics or control venues (e.g., ICRA, IROS, CDC, ACC).
